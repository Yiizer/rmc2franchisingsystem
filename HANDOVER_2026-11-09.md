**Security and reliability, this week:**
- **Sign-in limits:**
  - wrong passwords are counted per account (username and email share one count);
  - 60 attempts per IP per 15 minutes;
  - more than 300 wrong passwords site-wide pauses all sign-ins;
  - password checks no longer block the server.
- **Database:**
  - the audit tables are append-only;
  - the app's account is capped at 60 connections;
  - the "test / production" label cannot be changed by the app;
  - all 13 rollback scripts are guarded.
- **Incoming email:**
  - a sender counts as verified only with DMARC aligned to its own domain;
  - one bad email no longer blocks the rest, and oldest emails are read first;
  - size and attachment limits, and a check that a file really is Excel or PDF;
  - an inbox-health warning on Requests, with slower retries while the inbox keeps failing;
  - stuck imports are released after 10 minutes.
- **Excel files** are checked before they are opened, so a "zip bomb" is refused.
- **Handling locks:** an expired lock can no longer be revived by a late heartbeat.
- **Speed:** the search index refreshes in batches.
- **Paused database:** a paused Supabase project now shows a clear message instead of a 500.
